Works offline

No signal? It still answers.

Download a model once and MemoryAI replies without an internet connection: on a plane, on the underground, in the mountains. We tested it with airplane mode on: the app started, loaded its model and answered, with Wi-Fi and mobile data switched off.

Kid Mode

A safer AI for children

Turn on Kid Mode with a parent PIN, then hand over the phone. Your child can ask questions, learn and play with words, while the settings stay locked.

“I'm sorry, I can't talk about that. Please ask a parent or teacher.”

The gentle answer Kid Mode gives to questions about unsafe topics.

  • A parent PIN to turn it on and off, with a pause after too many wrong attempts.
  • The model and the settings are locked: no switching models, no system prompt, no clearing chats.
  • Filters for unsafe words and topics in all six languages of the app.
  • Templates made for kids, and memory switched off.

Kid Mode reduces exposure to unsafe content, but it does not eliminate it. Stay nearby while your child uses it.

Privacy

Your chats stay on your phone

Your conversations, documents and memories are processed and stored on your device. There is no account to create and there are no ads.

To improve the app we use usage statistics linked to a random app identifier, crash reports and, if you allow them, notifications. Never the content of your chats.

The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent and to whom: read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions

Is MemoryAI really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download a model, and for optional extras such as notifications. Once a model is on your phone, you can chat and ask about your documents without a connection.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 9 or later, and iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later. Models need storage space, a few gigabytes to start with, and phones with more memory (RAM) can run larger models.

Is Kid Mode completely safe?

No filter is perfect. Kid Mode reduces exposure to unsafe content, but it does not eliminate it. Stay nearby while your child uses it.

Where are my chats saved?

On your phone. If you use the backup option, a copy goes to your own Google Drive or iCloud.

Who makes MemoryAI?

Facchinits, an independent developer based in Italy. Questions and ideas are welcome at postmaster@facchinits.it.
